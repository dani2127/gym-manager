# Surface brief: apps/web

Development-only. Never copy this into source, markup or anything served to browsers.

Scope: public site `/` (Persuade), staff console `/desk` `/admin` `/trainer` `/account` (Operate), member PWA `/app` (Operate, mobile).
Audience: gym owners and managers in Ethiopia deciding on software; receptionists at a busy front desk at 6 am; members at the gym door with an Android phone.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A gym is a track: every branch runs in its own lane, every member wears a bib. We refuse the category default of a dark hero, a muscular stock model, a neon accent and three pricing cards.

OWN-WORLD: Stadium concrete ground (#E9EAE7), tartan-track red (#C2412D) with white lane lines, asphalt (#161816) for the console rail, infield green (#2E6B3C) for "allowed / open". Archivo at condensed width and heavy weight for bib numerals and headlines, Archivo at normal width for UI, Noto Sans Ethiopic for Amharic. Crisp 6px corners; the bib keeps 12px corners and four safety-pin holes.

STORY: Owners see their branches as lanes and the member pass as a bib, understand one system runs the whole business in birr, Amharic and Telegram, and start their gym. Staff find every tool where they expect it. Members show their bib at the door.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left, a two-line condensed headline and a working "start your gym" form (gym name, then continue to sign-up). Right, a large sample member bib with a real QR. Under both, full width, the tartan band with one lane per sample branch and occupancy markers.

FORM: Track & Bib, first of seven grounded candidates; user-chosen. seed: none (unavailable)

Signature interaction: lanes. Public: occupancy markers run to position once on load. Console: branch lanes show how far each branch is through today's opening hours, with a shared now-line.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
